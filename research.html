Machine Learning Research

A Counseling System to Predict the Study Path for Freshmen

BRAC University · Undergraduate Thesis · 2019

Designed and developed a machine learning recommendation system to help incoming university students identify suitable academic pathways based on their academic background, interests, skills, motivation, and values.

The research evaluated several supervised machine learning algorithms and recommendation techniques to compare prediction performance and improve decision support.
